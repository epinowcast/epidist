# Analytic delay summaries of the non-parametric family

Analytic delay summaries of the non-parametric family

## Usage

``` r
.np_delay_summaries(np)
```

## Arguments

- np:

  The `np` element of the family, holding the boundaries, the basis and
  its coefficients.

## Value

A list in the form of
[`.analytic_delay_summaries()`](https://epidist.epinowcast.org/reference/dot-analytic_delay_summaries.md).
